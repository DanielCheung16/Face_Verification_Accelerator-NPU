\subsection{Synthesis Critical Paths}
 
Three predicted critical paths in the current RTL:
\begin{enumerate}[nosep]
  \item \textbf{addr\_gen}: Two sequential multiplications (\texttt{y$\times$W+x},
        then \texttt{$\times$C+c}) in one cycle $\rightarrow$ split into 2-stage pipeline.
  \item \textbf{mfn\_activation}: PReLU multiplier between two combinational
        stages $\rightarrow$ insert pipeline register after multiplier.
  \item \textbf{mfn\_controller}: Large FSM combinational block
        $\rightarrow$ register outputs.
\end{enumerate}
