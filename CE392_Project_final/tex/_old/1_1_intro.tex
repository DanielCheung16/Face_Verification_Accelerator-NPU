\section{Project Overview}
%% ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 
This report the midterm progress of A fixed-point, MobileFaceNet-specific convolution frontend accelerator. Rather than implementing a fully configurable NPU, the design focuses on executing the convolutional frontend of MobileFaceNet with a fixed-point datapath and layer-by-layer control flow.
