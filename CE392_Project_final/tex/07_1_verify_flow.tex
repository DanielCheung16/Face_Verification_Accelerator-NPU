\subsection{Verification Flow}
\label{sec:verify:flow}

Verification is run in two layers. The model-level layer establishes that the INT8 fixed-point representation preserves face-verification accuracy on standard benchmarks; the hardware-level layer establishes that the RTL is bit-exact against the chosen C-model on every layer.

\subsubsection*{Model-Level: Face-Verification Benchmarks}

The model accuracy reference comes from QuantFace~\cite{9955645}, which trains a MobileFaceNet-class network in INT8 (w8a8) with synthetic-data low-bit quantization. We adopt its INT8 baseline directly and check that our hardware-spec fixed-point arithmetic matches the QuantFace PyTorch numbers on the standard face-verification benchmarks LFW, CFP, AgeDB-30, CALFW, CPLFW, IJB-B and IJB-C. QuantFace's published accuracy table (reproduced in Figure~\ref{fig:quantface_bench}) provides the reference per-benchmark numbers across multiple network backbones; the bottom MobileFaceNet (1.1\,M params) row at INT8 (w8a8) is the configuration our hardware runs.

\begin{figure}[H]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=0.95\textwidth]{Fig/peixing/benchmark.png}
  \caption[QuantFace face-verification benchmark suite.]{QuantFace face-verification benchmark accuracy across LFW, CFP, AgeDB-30, CALFW, CPLFW, and IJB-B/IJB-C. Source: QuantFace~\cite{9955645}. The MobileFaceNet w8a8 row (highlighted) is the configuration adopted by this design.}
  \label{fig:quantface_bench}
\end{figure}

Two of these benchmarks (LFW and AgeDB-30) are re-run with our hardware-spec fixed-point datapath to confirm the quantisation chosen for hardware does not regress against the QuantFace PyTorch reference. Results are in Table~\ref{tab:hw_spec_accuracy}: the LFW accuracy difference is 0.20\,\% and the AgeDB-30 difference is $-0.12\,\%$, both within the statistical error of the protocol.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\caption{Hardware-Spec Fixed-Point vs QuantFace PyTorch Reference}
\label{tab:hw_spec_accuracy}
\begin{tabular}{lcc}
\toprule
 & \textbf{LFW} & \textbf{AgeDB-30} \\
\midrule
QuantFace PyTorch (w8a8)        & 99.33\,\% & 95.12\,\% \\
HW-spec fixed-point (this work) & 99.13\,\% & 95.23\,\% \\
Accuracy drop                   & 0.20\,\%  & $-0.12\,\%$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

Accuracy here is measured under the standard verification protocol (image pair labelled $1$ if same person, $0$ otherwise).

\subsubsection*{Hardware-Level: Bit-Exact Equivalence}

Once the C-model is fixed against the QuantFace numbers, every subsequent stage is verified by direct comparison against the C-model golden hex vectors layer by layer:
\[
\text{PyTorch (INT8)} \;\rightarrow\; \text{C-model} \;\rightarrow\; \text{per-layer golden hex} \;\rightarrow\; \text{implementation under test} \;\rightarrow\; \text{bit diff}.
\]
The C-model is bit-exact against the chosen INT8 arithmetic and is the canonical reference. Table~\ref{tab:verify_matrix} summarises the coverage matrix for both implementation tracks.
