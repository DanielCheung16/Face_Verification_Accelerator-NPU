\section{Engineering Standards}
\label{sec:standards}

Table~\ref{tab:standards} lists the engineering standards and reference specifications used throughout the design.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\caption{Engineering Standards Applied}
\label{tab:standards}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{l l X}
\toprule
\headrow \textbf{Domain} & \textbf{Standard} & \textbf{Where used} \\
\midrule
HDL                & IEEE 1800-2017 (SystemVerilog)         & All RTL modules (synthesisable subset) \\
HDL                & IEEE 1364-2005 (Verilog 2005)          & OpenRAM-emitted SRAM behavioural models \\
Library exchange   & LEF/DEF (Si2 OpenAccess)               & Standard-cell + SRAM physical abstracts; Innovus floorplan import/export \\
Timing models      & Liberty (.lib, Synopsys-de-facto)      & Cell delays for Genus / Innovus / Xcelium \\
Delay annotation   & IEEE 1497 (SDF)                        & Post-route SDF backannotation for gate-level signoff \\
Constraints        & Synopsys SDC                           & \texttt{constraints\_v3.sdc}: clock, IO delay, false paths \\
Reference floats   & IEEE 754 binary32                      & PyTorch baseline + C-model fp32 reference \\
Fixed-point        & Q10 (data/weights), Q20 (accumulator)  & internal datapath \\
Process design kit & NCSU FreePDK45                         & ASIC standard-cell library + DRC rules \\
Memory IP          & OpenRAM (open-source)                  & 192$\times$64 act\_buf, 512$\times$40 psum SRAMs \\

\paragraph{Compliance notes.} The RTL is written in the synthesisable subset of SystemVerilog 2017 (no \texttt{assert}, no \texttt{$dynamic$}, no \texttt{class}, limited use of \texttt{interface}). All cell timing checks (setup/hold/recovery/removal) follow the standard SDF semantics. The LEF patching script \texttt{fix\_sram\_lef.py} preserves the LEF syntax conventions specified in the Si2 LEF/DEF Language Reference; no extension keywords are introduced.
